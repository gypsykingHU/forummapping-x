# Feed analysis, Oct 1 - Nov 7 2026 (987 posts, from reports/posts-2026-11-07.csv)

## Baselines (median impressions per post; means are dominated by a few viral hits)
- Original historical map: ~1,600 (Oct 1-17: 1,990; Nov: 1,598). Empires & borders 2,250, Wars 1,567, Royal houses 1,209.
- Claude data map: ~450 (Nov: 396). Economy & society 540, Demographics & language 490. One historical-themed Claude Map (1914 mottos) hit 1.3M impressions = 49% of ALL impressions in the period.
- OWID map (retired): ~400. Fact: ~410, flat across hours (fell ~480 -> ~370 as facts/day rose 5 -> 12).
- Thread opener: median 892, mean 3,362. Thread tail posts: median 114 (15%+ of posts, 2.8% of impressions).
- Top 5% of posts = 85% of impressions; top 10 posts = 71%.

## Why viewership dipped
- Week of Oct 26: 145k impressions vs 267-291k in early/mid Oct (excluding the 1.6M viral week). No viral hit, and the map slots shifted to Claude data maps (~450) from historical originals (~1,600).
- Volume (19 -> 40 posts/day) shows only a mild per-post effect: originals -20%, facts -20%. Not the main cause.
- Posts <90 min after the previous one: map median ~530-670; 90+ min gap: 710-910 (noisy).
- No strong hour-of-day effect (overnight medians ~ daytime).
